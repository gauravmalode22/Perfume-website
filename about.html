How Scenta began

We started Scenta because buying perfume online is hard. You cannot smell a screen, and most product pages list ten notes without telling you how a bottle will actually feel on a Tuesday morning or at a wedding.

So we describe every fragrance in plain language, who it suits and when to wear it, and we offer small sample sizes so you can live with a scent before you commit to the full bottle.
